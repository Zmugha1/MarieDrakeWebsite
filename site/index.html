The Drake Law Firm, P.C. is a full-service family law firm that focuses on divorce, child custody, child support, spousal maintenance, common law marriage, allocation of parental responsibilities, post-decree modifications, prenuptial and postnuptial agreements, and paternity.

About The Drake Law Firm P.C.

Marie Drake is the founder of The Drake Law Firm, P.C., which she established in 2011 to provide strategic, solution-focused legal representation in Family Law, Divorce, Child Custody, Common Law Marriage, Spousal Support, Allocations of Parental Responsibility, Prenuptial Agreements, Mediation and Child Support. Practicing law since 1999, Marie is dedicated to active listening and a proactive approach, ensuring her clients receive personalized and effective guidance in both litigation and transactional matters. Under her leadership, our family law attorneys are committed to helping clients navigate these complex legal challenges with confidence.

Practice Areas

Divorce

Navigating divorce requires skilled legal counsel to protect your interests and future. Our team provides experienced divorce representation and guidance through every stage of the dissolution process.

Child Custody

Your relationship with your children matters most. We're committed to protecting your child custody rights and securing parenting arrangements that serve your family's best interests.

Child Support

Ensuring fair financial support for your children is critical. We handle child support adjustments in Colorado to reflect your family's current circumstances and needs.

Alimony

Spousal support can significantly impact your financial future. Our attorneys provide strategic counsel on spousal maintenance and alimony matters whether you're seeking or contesting support.

Property Division

Dividing assets and debts requires careful attention to Colorado law. We ensure equitable marital property division in divorce to protect your financial foundation.

Paternity

Legal recognition of parentage carries important rights and responsibilities. Our firm assists with establishing paternity and parental rights for both mothers and fathers.

Prenup & Postnup

Protecting assets and clarifying expectations strengthens relationships. Our attorneys draft comprehensive prenuptial and postnuptial agreements tailored to your unique situation.

Adoption

Building your family through adoption is a profound journey. We provide adoption services and legal representation to help you navigate the process with confidence.
